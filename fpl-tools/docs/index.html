Difficulty is built from each opponent's own attack and defence rating from last season, adjusted for venue — not FPL's official pre-season FDR, which is set before a ball is kicked and goes stale by October. Promoted sides carry a first-season prior.

Captain

Ceiling and blank chance come from a points distribution, not just the average. In your classic league a blank costs you rank against everyone at once, so the steady pick is usually right. In head-to-head you only need to beat one manager, which makes a high ceiling on a player your opponent doesn't own worth more than it looks.

Vice-captain

The vice only scores if your captain plays no minutes at all — not if he plays badly. So this is insurance, priced at the chance your captain is absent times what the vice would score. Kickoff order doesn't matter: FPL applies the switch automatically once the gameweek ends, so a vice playing Friday still covers a captain who sits out on Sunday. The one thing worth avoiding is a vice in the same match as your captain — a postponement takes out both at once.
